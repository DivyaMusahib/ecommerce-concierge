# ShopMate E-Commerce FAQ

## Return Policy
You can return any physical item within 30 days of delivery. The item must be in its original packaging and unused condition. Digital products (software licenses, subscription codes) are non-refundable once activated. To initiate a return, contact our support team with your order ID. A return pickup will be scheduled within 2 business days. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the item is received at our warehouse.

## Shipping Times
Standard shipping usually takes 5-7 business days within India. Expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs Rs.99 extra. Same-day delivery is available in select metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune) for orders placed before 11 AM. Overnight shipping is available for select items and costs Rs.199 extra. All orders are dispatched within 24 hours of placement on business days.

## Warranty Information
All electronics come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects. You can purchase an extended 3-year warranty at checkout for an additional fee (typically 8-12% of product price). Warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, theft, or accidental damage. To claim warranty, contact us with your order ID and a description or photo of the defect.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to India, the US, Canada, UK, UAE, Singapore, and Australia. International shipping times vary between 10-21 business days depending on destination and customs. International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the buyer's responsibility. We use DHL and FedEx for international shipments. Tracking is available for all international orders.

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking (all major Indian banks), and PayPal for international orders. We offer EMI options with 0% interest on select credit cards for orders above Rs.5000. We do not accept cash on delivery for orders above Rs.10,000. Cryptocurrency payments are not accepted.

## Order Cancellation
You can cancel an order for free if it has not yet been dispatched. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled - you will need to return it after delivery. To cancel, go to your orders page or ask our AI assistant. Pre-paid order cancellations are refunded within 5-7 business days. COD orders cancelled before dispatch incur no charge.

## Order Tracking
You can track your order using the order ID provided in your confirmation email. Track directly by asking our AI assistant about your order ID. You will receive SMS and email notifications at each stage: Order Confirmed, Dispatched, Out for Delivery, and Delivered. If tracking shows no update for more than 3 business days, contact support.

## Exchange Policy
Exchanges are allowed within 30 days of delivery for items of equal or higher value (you pay the price difference). The item must be unused and in original packaging. Exchange pickups are free for defective or wrong items. For size or preference changes, a Rs.99 exchange fee applies. Digital products cannot be exchanged.

## Damaged or Wrong Items
If you receive a damaged item or a wrong product, please contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free reverse pickup and either replace the item or issue a full refund including original shipping charges. We apologize for any inconvenience caused by such errors.

## EMI and Finance Options
EMI is available on orders above Rs.5,000 with most major credit cards including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak. EMI tenures available are 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, and 24 months. Zero-cost EMI means you pay the product price in equal monthly installments with no additional interest. A processing fee may apply depending on the bank. Bajaj Finserv and ZestMoney cardless EMI are also accepted.

## Gift Wrapping and Special Packaging
Gift wrapping is available for Rs.49 per item and can be selected at checkout. You can add a personalized gift message at no extra cost. Gift-wrapped items are delivered in premium packaging with a printed message card. Corporate gift orders of 10 or more items receive complimentary gift packaging.

## Account and Privacy
Your personal data is protected under our Privacy Policy compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023. We do not sell your data to third parties. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting support. Passwords are stored securely using industry-standard encryption. You can update your shipping address, email, and phone number from the account settings page.

## Loyalty Points and Rewards
ShopMate Points are earned at 1 point per Rs.10 spent. Points can be redeemed at checkout where 100 points equals Rs.10 discount. Points expire after 12 months of inactivity. You earn bonus points on your birthday month (2x points) and during sale events.

## Bulk and Business Orders
For bulk orders of 10 or more units or business and corporate purchases, contact our B2B team at business@shopmate.in for volume discounts up to 20%. GST invoices are provided for all orders. Business accounts can request credit terms of up to 30 days for verified companies.

## Product Authenticity
All products sold on ShopMate are 100% genuine and sourced from authorized distributors and brand-authorized sellers. We are an authorized reseller for Apple, Samsung, Sony, LG, Dell, HP, and Logitech. Each product comes with a manufacturer's authenticity certificate where applicable. If you suspect a product is counterfeit, contact support immediately.

## Coupon Codes and Discounts
Coupon codes can be applied at checkout. Only one coupon code can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be combined with other promotional offers unless explicitly stated. NEWUSER coupon gives 15% off and is valid for first-time customers only. SAVE10 gives 10% off on orders above Rs.5,000. FLAT500 gives Rs.500 flat off on orders above Rs.10,000. Expired coupons will show an error message.

## Tech Support
For technical issues with purchased products, contact our tech support team. We offer free remote troubleshooting for software-related issues within 30 days of purchase. For hardware issues, we can arrange an authorized service center visit. Tech support hours are Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST.

## Delivery Areas and PIN Codes
We deliver to all PIN codes across India. Delivery availability and timelines may vary for remote areas. Enter your PIN code on the product page to check delivery availability and estimated delivery date before ordering. For areas outside our standard network, delivery may take 7-10 business days.

## Subscription and Auto-Renewal
Some products like antivirus software or cloud storage may come with auto-renewal subscriptions. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your account settings under Subscriptions. We send a reminder email 7 days before any auto-renewal charge. Cancelling a subscription does not refund already-paid amounts.

## Customer Support Contact
Our AI assistant is available 24/7 for instant help. For complex issues, human support agents are available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST. Contact options: Live chat (fastest response), Email at support@shopmate.in with response within 24 hours, and phone support at 1800-XXX-XXXX toll-free from 9 AM to 6 PM. For urgent escalations, the AI assistant can raise a HIGH priority ticket for a 2-hour response guarantee.
